BookMyDesk – Smart Library Desk Reservation & Occupancy Management System

BookMyDesk is a smart library management solution designed to help students find, reserve, and manage study spaces efficiently while improving the utilization of library resources.
The project aims to address common challenges in university libraries, including difficulty finding available desks, overcrowded study areas, unattended belongings occupying seats, and inefficient space allocation.
By combining AI-powered image processing, real-time occupancy monitoring, and an intuitive mobile application, BookMyDesk envisions a smarter, fairer, and more accessible library experience for students and library administrators.

Key Objectives

Smart Desk Reservations: Allow students to browse study areas, check desk availability, and reserve suitable workspaces.
Real-Time Occupancy Tracking: Provide visibility into available, occupied, and reserved desks.
AI-Powered Detection: Explore computer vision techniques to distinguish between actively occupied desks and spaces held by unattended belongings.
Interactive Library Maps: Help users navigate library floors and locate available study spaces.
Efficient Space Management: Reduce unused reservations, optimize seating capacity, and improve resource allocation.
User-Friendly Experience: Simplify the reservation process and reduce the time students spend searching for study spaces.

Technology & Approach

The project explores the integration of mobile application development, computer vision, and data-driven occupancy management. Its proposed architecture incorporates AI-based object detection, image processing, and desk reservation workflows to support intelligent library operations.

Our Vision

Find your space. Reserve your desk. Focus on what matters.
BookMyDesk aims to transform traditional university libraries into smarter, more efficient study environments, helping students spend less time searching for a desk and more time focusing on their studies.
